# RepoLoop code of conduct

## Our standard

RepoLoop welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Participation should be professional, constructive, and focused on making repository-agent tooling safer and more useful.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving and accepting specific, evidence-based feedback;
- assuming good intent while addressing harmful impact;
- respecting differing experience, identity, and perspective;
- keeping technical disagreements about the work rather than the person;
- protecting private repository content, credentials, and personal information;
- accepting maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation against a reporter.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication, and public representation of the RepoLoop community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [joseph.ke.han@gmail.com](mailto:joseph.ke.han@gmail.com). Include relevant links or screenshots, the date, and a concise description. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporter privacy where practical, and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include clarification, a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

False reports made in bad faith may themselves violate this policy. Disagreement, criticism, and evidence-based security review are not harassment.
